# Code of conduct

The repositories of JohnnyMaxK follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) ([version française](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/fr/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, criticise ideas and not people, accept constructive feedback, and never harass anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **johnny@jmk.studio**. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour.
